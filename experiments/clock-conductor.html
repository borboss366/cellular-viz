Open the page

  • http://192.168.8.164:8790/ plays audio only. The clock needs http://localhost:8790/ through the tunnel: ssh -L 8790:localhost:8790 puggie@192.168.8.164

Play a track

  1. In the Playlist box, click a track name.
  2. Click "Load to NEXT".
  3. Wait until the deck shows the track name (not "loading").
  4. Push "Play / pause" or Space. The deck changes to LIVE. This also starts the audio.

Go to the next track

  1. Click the next track name. Click "Load to NEXT". Wait for the name.
  2. Push "Crossfade" or N. The old deck stops after the fade time.

Fix the clock (Shift + key)

  • Read the wheel: top = the clock beat. The line under it names the key to push.
  • Main lobe at the bottom (half a beat off): flip, Shift+F.
  • Tempo is half: x2, Shift+D. Tempo is double: /2, Shift+H. Push x2 or /2 one time only.
  • x2 and /2 hold the octave. Press /2 again to release it (x2 again after x2). The tempo goes back at the next bar line.
  • Small offset: -10 or +10, Shift+, or Shift+.
  • Wrong "1": set 1, Shift+1, on the downbeat.
  • Wrong tempo: tap, Shift+A, on each beat, less than 2 s apart. One to three taps only move the beat.
  • Tap 4 to 8 times on the beat. Small timing errors are averaged. The taps set the tempo and release the octave hold. A red flash means a missed or a double tap: tap again.
  • reset, Shift+0, counts the bar again. It does not clear the tempo or the octave hold.
  • If the tempo or the octave hold is wrong and a tap does not fix it, push Shift+R (restart). The clock starts again; the music does not stop.
  • If that does not help: reload the page (Cmd+R), then load and play the track again.
  • K shows or hides the key lamp (the reference beats).

Marks and the bar

  • Select a name in the Marks list. Push M on that moment. [ and ] move bar1 by one beat.
  • Shift+S confirms the bar. Push it on the "1" while the bar 1 lamp is on.
  • Marks need the LIVE deck playing a track with a grid.

After the sitting

  • Push "Download log". Then stop the server on the box.
  • Do not push K (answer-key lamp) in a scored run.
  • Push Help or ? to close this panel.
