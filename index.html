cellular-viz

Single-file WebGL2 cellular automata experiments. Touch the field to draw.

01 · Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion Coral, mitosis, worms, fingerprint. f/k sliders, finger drawing. 02 · Lenia Orbium gliders, swarm, soup, bloom. R/T/μ/σ sliders, paint and erase. 03 · Lenia, audio-reactive Orbium on the beat: mic or a music file drives flash, μ nudge and blob injection. BPM in the HUD. 04 · Lenia on a metronome Synthetic kick/snare/hat/pad on the Web Audio clock: blobs, flash, shimmer and hue drift. BPM, swing, 16-step patterns. 05 · Flow Lenia Mass-conserving Lenia: mass flows instead of appearing, so it never fills up or dies out. Randomize μ/σ, stir with a finger, flow-direction view. 06 · Lenia + effects 04's metronome Lenia with kick-pumped bloom, emboss and sheen, cosine palettes, MilkDrop-style trails and a kick bulge. Looks: clean, glow, chrome, dream, pulse. 07 · Particle Lenia Points on an open plane follow the energy gradient of Particle Lenia (Mordvintsev et al. 2022) and gather into rotors and rings; 04's metronome drives repulsion, growth, flash and hue. Presets from the user's 55-swipe picks: small-ring, dense, loose, hard-rep. 08 · Blend A playlist of experiment pages crossfaded on the downbeat: fade (the user's pick), dissolve, density mask, iris, a Gray-Scott coral mask, beat cuts and screen/colour/difference layering. The first sketch of the live set orchestrator. 09 · Riemann zeta Millennium problems #1: the zeta function as a colour wheel per point; every zero is a rainbow pinwheel on the critical line. Glides up the strip on the beat. Fullscreen for a laptop. 10 · Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer The first BSD page: a point hops along an elliptic curve by the exact group law on the beat (add on beats, double on the downbeat), over the curve's solutions mod p, coloured by a_p. 17 and 19 grew out of it. 11 · Riemann, the show 09 take two, five scenes on the beat: a chase-cam ride along the zeta ribbon, the zeros as runway lights, a Z(t) oscilloscope, the primes carved out by the zeros at the drop, and a canyon flight over |ζ|. Every zero lands on a downbeat. 17 · BSD Skater 10 take two: skate an elliptic curve as a neon half-pipe, chill pace. Tricks are the group law (ollie along a chord and flip to P+Q, tangent grind to 2P, big air at the point at infinity), over a city of lights for the curve mod p. 19 Skate Park won the grade; this is its ancestor. 18 · Little Bang (Yang–Mills, take two) 12 rebuilt as a collider show, all particles: gold nuclei smash on the downbeat, a white-hot quark–gluon fireball flows and hadronizes into thousands of curling detector tracks, jets fire on the snares, colour strings snap into white mesons, and one jet in each pair gets swallowed by the plasma, leaving a glowing Mach cone. Beamline: ride the beam pipe of a ring collider past magnets on the beat, stop at the collision point as the bunches cross on the downbeat, and watch the calorimeter light up. 19 · Skate Park (BSD, about the path) 17's skater on wilder ramps, slow and close: a rail wound round the complex torus of an elliptic curve as a (2,3) → (3,5) → (5,8) knot, a pipe that morphs until the egg pinches into the main branch on the drop, and a free-ride coaster with corkscrews. Slow tricks only at section changes and drops. 12 · Mass Gap (Yang–Mills) Millennium problems: coloured ripples race free in the build-up and lock into breathing white blobs when the gap switches on at the drop; a flux tube that stretches and snaps; a glueball equaliser with an empty gap band; a QCD lava lamp. 13 · Flow Lab (Navier–Stokes) A real incompressible fluid on the GPU plus curl-noise currents. Recipes: a Kármán vortex street locked to the BPM by the Strouhal number, Kelvin–Helmholtz billows, a smoke plume, ink drops, a stream through rocks. Tweak, 🎲 randomise, save, morph between recipes over N bars, drag to stir. 14 · Round Point (Poincaré) Perelman's idea in two scenes: a glowing curve flowing by its own curvature, healing every kick bump and shrinking to a round point on the section's last beat (its area falls at exactly 2π), and a dumbbell surface whose neck pinches off on the drop, leaving two round pieces. 23 · Shape Shifter (Poincaré) A ceramic mug melts into a kettle, a doughnut, a ring, always with one hole, because a mug and a doughnut are the same shape to a topologist. On the drop the handle pinches and snaps (Perelman's surgery) and the body rounds out into a sphere. The surface bumps to the drums, its colour shows the curvature, and the camera dives through the handle. 25 · Shape Shifter II (Poincaré) 23, faster and wilder: thirty shapes with 0 to 3 holes (pretzel, glasses, amphora, a trefoil knot) change on every downbeat, faster as the song builds. Holes snap off and grow back on the phrase drops, the shape squashes on the kick, and the camera orbits, skims the surface and slaloms through every hole. 16c · 27 Neon Lines (Hodge) The Clebsch cubic surface as an iridescent soap film, carrying all 27 of its straight lines as neon lasers that pulse on the beat, with its 10 Eckardt points as gold diamonds. Symmetries glide the surface through new shapes and land it back on itself with the lines swapped. The build-up adds lines 3 → 7 → 15 → 27, each new pair born as twins at the drop of a wall.
